The garden

Projects, kept the way a garden is kept rather than the way a portfolio is: some things here are years old and still going, some were planted last month and may not survive the winter.

  • An electric momo steamer

    Designed, welded and tested across 2023 and 2024

    A steamer that loses less heat than the one in every kitchen I grew up around. I insulated the body, moved it from gas to electricity, and measured how much steam was escaping before and after. It cooks the same. It costs less to run. That is the whole argument for electric cooking in one pot.

    Thermal designFabricationElectric cooking

    still growing well

  • A go-kart built around a 98cc engine

    Built between 2019 and 2020

    Four of us, one two-stroke engine, and a workshop floor. We built the frame, broke it, measured why, and built it again until the thing would hold together at 37 km/h. I learned more about tolerances from that chain than from any exam I have ever sat.

    Machine designFabricationTesting

    Still growing well

  • Energy plans for towns that don't have one yet

    Ongoing since 2024, at Multiscope Solution

    Municipal energy planning is mostly walking around asking people what they burn and when. Then it becomes a demand assessment, then a spreadsheet, then a set of measures a mayor can actually sign off on. I also run audits of buildings and factories, which is the same job at a smaller scale.

    Energy auditsDemand assessmentMunicipal planning

    Tended weekly

  • What a tonne of carbon should cost in Nepal

    Research and documentation support, UNDP-supported work

    Desk research and drafting for a national carbon pricing policy. The engineering question is easy compared with the political one, which is who pays, how much, and what happens to the money afterwards.

    Carbon pricingPolicy researchDrafting

    Tended weekly

  • A sculpture made of dead gearboxes

    February 2024, and whenever the scrap pile gets tall

    Chains, gears and bearings that were already rubbish, welded into something that was not. It won the audience vote, which I maintain is the only prize that counts. There is a second one half-finished under my bed.

    Junk artWeldingUnfinished

    newly planted
